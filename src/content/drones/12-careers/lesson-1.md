## A Data-Intensive Future

Precision and smart agriculture technologies are enabling substantially higher output while using inputs such as crop protection products and irrigation water more efficiently, thereby lowering production costs and improving farm profitability. At the same time, AI has the potential to act as a leveler by delivering scalable, farm‑size‑agnostic decision tools and making advanced analytics affordable across diverse production systems. Rapid innovation in sensing platforms, unmanned aerial systems, satellite remote sensing, and advanced agricultural machinery is transforming agriculture into a data‑intensive, technology‑driven sector.
