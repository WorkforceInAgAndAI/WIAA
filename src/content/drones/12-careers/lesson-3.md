Consider different career options and the college path that will give you the most information and prepare you for a career of interest.

## How Skills Connect

<div class="impact-callout">
  <h4>Skills Cross Over More Than You would Expect</h4>
  <p>Skills and knowledge in genetics are useful for a data scientist working with genetic data. Mechanical engineering knowledge is useful for an agronomist when designing studies that implement new technology.</p>
</div>
