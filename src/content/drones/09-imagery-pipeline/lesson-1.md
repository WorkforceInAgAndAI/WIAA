## The Mission


Your job is to determine the best performing genotype in the field. You will use drone imagery and image analysis to fly your field, collect data, analyze data, and draw conclusions.



## Flight Planning


When we set up our flight, we need to consider the quality of the data that is captured. If low spatial resolution is acceptable, sensors can fly higher and get through the field faster, but if detailed data is required, then the drone must fly closer to the ground to capture more details. Lower altitude flights take more time because the drone needs to make more passes over the field to ensure all parts of the field are captured.

<p class="section-intro">Spatial resolution describes the amount of area captured by a sensor. A high spatial resolution means a small ground area is captured by a single pixel, while a low spatial resolution means a larger area of the ground is captured by a single pixel. </p>
