## What Is NDVI?


NDVI is one of many vegetation index (VI) formulas that use specific reflectance values to determine plant health. NDVI stands for normalized difference vegetative index. 



<div class="formula-box">

&#x20; <span class="formula">NDVI = (NIR - Red) / (NIR + Red)</span>

</div>



## Interpreting NDVI



<ul class="interpretation-list">

&#x20; <li>A <strong>high NDVI</strong> signals healthy plants</li>

&#x20; <li>A <strong>low NDVI</strong> signals stressed plants</li>

&#x20; <li>A <strong>very low NDVI</strong> indicates no plants are present and is a sign of bare soil</li>

</ul>
