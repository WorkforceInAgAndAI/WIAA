## What Is Part 107?

Small UASs operate in national airspace so drone pilots must abide by a set of regulations to ensure drone operations are safe. If you are flying a drone for a **commercial purpose** that **weighs less than 55 pounds**, you are subject to Part 107 guidelines set in Title 14, Chapter 1, Subchapter F of the Code of Federal Regulations by the U.S. Government and **must maintain an up-to-date Remote Pilot Certification.**
