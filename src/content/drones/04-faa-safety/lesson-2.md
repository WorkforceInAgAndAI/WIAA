To be eligible to operate a drone under the Part 107 regulations, you must meet the following criteria and follow the steps outlined. 

<div class="steps-container">
  <div class="step-card">
    <div class="step-header">
      <div class="step-number">✓</div>
      <h3>Basic Requirements</h3>
    </div>
    <div class="step-body">
      <ul>
        <li>16 years old</li>
        <li>Fluent in English</li>
        <li>No interfering mental or physical conditions</li>
      </ul>
    </div>
  </div>

  <div class="step-card">
    <div class="step-header">
      <div class="step-number">1</div>
      <h3>Obtain an FTN</h3>
    </div>
    <div class="step-body">
      <ul>
        <li>Get an FAA Tracking Number (FTN) before testing</li>
      </ul>
    </div>
  </div>

  <div class="step-card">
    <div class="step-header">
      <div class="step-number">2</div>
      <h3>Pass the Knowledge Test</h3>
    </div>
    <div class="step-body">
      <ul>
        <li>Pass the Unmanned Aircraft General – Small (UAG) Knowledge Test</li>
      </ul>
    </div>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="impact-callout">
  <h4>After You're Certified</h4>
  <p>Once you become a Part 107 Pilot, you must ensure the drone you plan to operate is registered with the FAA and is set up with a Remote ID or is only flown in a FAA-recognized Identification Area (FRIA).</p>
</div>
