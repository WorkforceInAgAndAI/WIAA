When flying, a drone operator must keep a close eye on their and the drone’s surroundings. Weather can have major effects on drone performance as well as data collection or application quality. Reviewing the drone manual to determine the capabilities and tolerances for various weather conditions is vital to the safe operations of drones. 

## Reading a METAR

Pilots should pay close attention to wind direction, wind speed, precipitation, visibility, dew point, temperature, cloud cover, and time of day. This information can be found in a Meteorological Aerodrome Report (METAR) formatted as a series of abbreviated letters and numbers to signify local weather conditions.

## Pre-Flight Inspection
Before flying, drone pilots must also closely inspect the UAS they are planning to fly. Check all components and connections between the UAV and the remote controller to ensure the drone is safe and will remain safe to operate.
