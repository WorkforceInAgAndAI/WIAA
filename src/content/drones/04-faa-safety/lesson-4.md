When operating a drone, many regulations must be met to ensure the safety of the operator and the drone. Just like operating a vehicle on land, it is important to operate vehicles in the air safely and with purpose. More information on drone operations under the Part 107 FAA regulations can be found at <a href="https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-14/chapter-I/subchapter-F/part-107" target="\_blank" rel="noopener">ecfr.gov</a>.

## 10 Key Rules



<div class="rules-timeline">

&#x20; <button class="rule-item" data-rule="1">

&#x20;   <div class="rule-marker"><span class="rule-num">1</span></div>

&#x20;   <div class="rule-content">

&#x20;     <h3>Keep Drone in Sight - 14 CFR Part 107.31 
</h3>

&#x20;     <p class="rule-description">You need to be able to see the drone the whole time when 
you are flying. You need to know the location, altitude, 
direction, attitude, surrounding airspace and hazard potential.
</p>

&#x20;   </div>

&#x20; </button>



&#x20; <button class="rule-item" data-rule="2">

&#x20;   <div class="rule-marker"><span class="rule-num">2</span></div>

&#x20;   <div class="rule-content">

&#x20;     <h3>Give Way to Planes - 14 CFR Part 107.37
</h3>

&#x20;     <p class="rule-description"> If you are flying and see another plane flying by, you are 
responsible for moving out of the way from the plane or 
any other flying aircraft. You do not have the right of way.
</p>

&#x20;   </div>

&#x20; </button>



&#x20; <button class="rule-item" data-rule="3">

&#x20;   <div class="rule-marker"><span class="rule-num">3</span></div>

&#x20;   <div class="rule-content">

&#x20;     <h3>Fly One Drone at a Time - 14 CFR Part 107.35
</h3>

&#x20;     <p class="rule-description">One person can only fly one drone. Two people cannot 
fly one drone, and one person cannot fly two drones. 
</p>

&#x20;   </div>

&#x20; </button>



&#x20; <button class="rule-item" data-rule="4">

&#x20;   <div class="rule-marker"><span class="rule-num">4</span></div>

&#x20;   <div class="rule-content">

&#x20;     <h3>No Reckless Flying - 14 CFR Part 107.23
</h3>

&#x20;     <p class="rule-description">Do not fly recklessly. Just like operating a car, 
drones need to be flown carefully and purposefully. </p>

&#x20;   </div>

&#x20; </button>



&#x20; <button class="rule-item" data-rule="5">

&#x20;   <div class="rule-marker"><span class="rule-num">5</span></div>

&#x20;   <div class="rule-content">

&#x20;     <h3>Maximum Altitude 14 CFR Part 107.51(b)
</h3>

&#x20;     <div class="rule-details">

&#x20;       <ul>

&#x20;         <li>              You are not allowed to fly more than 400 feet above ground. 
</li>

&#x20;         <li>If you are 
               flying above a building, you are allowed to fly 400 feet above the building only 
                  if you have a waver. 
</li>

&#x20;         <li>You must be a minimum of 500 feet below the clouds and 2000 feet
 horizontally away from the clouds. 
</li>

&#x20;       </ul>

&#x20;     </div>

&#x20;   </div>

&#x20; </button>



&#x20; <button class="rule-item" data-rule="6">

&#x20;   <div class="rule-marker"><span class="rule-num">6</span></div>

&#x20;   <div class="rule-content">

&#x20;     <h3>Maximum Speed - 14 CFR Part 107.51(a)
</h3>

&#x20;     <p class="rule-description">Do not fly more than 100 miles per hour 
at any time for any reason.  
</p>

&#x20;   </div>

&#x20; </button>



&#x20; <button class="rule-item" data-rule="7">

&#x20;   <div class="rule-marker"><span class="rule-num">7</span></div>

&#x20;   <div class="rule-content">

&#x20;     <h3>Flight Time - 14 CFR Part 107.29</h3>

&#x20;     <p class="rule-description">              You may fly your drone for 30 minutes before the sunrise and 30 
         minutes after sunset during civil twilight. If you have specialized 
   FAA approved lighting on the drone, then you may request 
   exceptions to this regulation. 
</p>

&#x20;   </div>

&#x20; </button>



&#x20; <button class="rule-item" data-rule="8">

&#x20;   <div class="rule-marker"><span class="rule-num">8</span></div>

&#x20;   <div class="rule-content">

&#x20;     <h3>Maximum Distance Between Drone and Pilot - 14 CFR Part 107.51(c) 
</h3>

&#x20;     <p class="rule-description">You must always be able to see the drone. You'll often lose sight before it's 3 miles away — bring it back closer if that happens.</p>

&#x20;   </div>

&#x20; </button>



&#x20; <button class="rule-item" data-rule="9">

&#x20;   <div class="rule-marker"><span class="rule-num">9</span></div>

&#x20;   <div class="rule-content">

&#x20;     <h3>Do Not Fly Over People - 14 CFR Part 107.39
</h3>

&#x20;     <p class="rule-description">Do not fly over a group of people at a sporting event or a 
crowd of people in any other setting. You can fly over
  individuals operating the UAV or protected from falling UAS.
See 14 CFR Subpart D for more information. 
</p>

&#x20;   </div>

&#x20; </button>



&#x20; <button class="rule-item" data-rule="10">

&#x20;   <div class="rule-marker"><span class="rule-num">10</span></div>

&#x20;   <div class="rule-content">

&#x20;     <h3>Do Not Fly From Moving Vehicles - 14 CFR Part 107.25
</h3>

&#x20;     <p class="rule-description">You are also not allowed to fly a drone when you 
are in a moving vehicle like a boat or a car unless you 
are in a sparsely populated area.
</p>

&#x20;   </div>

&#x20; </button>

</div>
