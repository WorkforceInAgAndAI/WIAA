Sensors are devices that record measurements. Some sensors specialize in measuring dimensions of objects such as weight, or size. Others may measure object quality, such as speed, temperature, or light reflectance. 

<p class="section-intro">We can record sensor outputs to learn how objects change or develop. Sensors are valuable because they allow data to be collected where humans cannot. Sensors can be deployed at any time, allowing measurements to be recorded 24/7. </p>

Sensors discussed in these learning modules target object light reflectance, and some thermal reflectance. RGB, multispectral, hyperspectral, and LiDAR are all sensors that can be used to detect light, however they do so in different ways depending on their sensitivity to electromagnetic radiation.
