You are a farmer with a 350-acre soybean field. Today, while walking the field, you noticed a Japanese beetle infestation in the northeast corner of your field. You do not know if the Japanese beetles are only in the northeast corner of the field where you looked, or if they have spread to other parts of the field. Regardless, you need to produce a solution to remove the Japanese beetles from the soybean field.

<p class="section-intro">You have <strong>432 hours</strong> before you leave on vacation, and a <strong>$75,000 budget</strong>. Every day you spend managing these pests costs an additional <strong>$1,000 in yield losses</strong>. Your solution needs at least <strong>75% accuracy</strong> to be considered reliable. The best solution controls both pests effectively while spending the least money and the least time.</p>
