Rovers are robots that drive along the ground and have cameras. Rovers have multiple qualities that give them a unique perspective in the field.

## What Makes Rovers Different

<div class="benefits-grid">
  <div class="benefit-card">
    <div class="benefit-number">1</div>
    <h4>Canopy-Level Cameras</h4>
    <p>Cameras are located at plant canopy level.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="benefit-card">
    <div class="benefit-number">2</div>
    <h4>Programmable Driving</h4>
    <p>Programmable driving allows the rover to move on its own..</p>
  </div>
  <div class="benefit-card">
    <div class="benefit-number">3</div>
    <h4>Programmable Scouting</h4>
    <p>Programmable scouting allows the rover to follow the same path at the same time each day.</p>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="impact-callout">
  <h4>Where Rovers Excel</h4>
  <p>Because of these characteristics, rovers can be used in agriculture for tasks such as measuring plant height and structure, monitoring individual plant or leaf health, scouting for insects, weeds, and diseases on a plant scale in precise locations, counting plants, measuring plant density, and yield estimations. </p>
</div>
