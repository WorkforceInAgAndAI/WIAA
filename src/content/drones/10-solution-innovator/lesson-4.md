AI is a computer model trained to recognize patterns in data. 

<p class="section-intro">For example, an AI can be trained to recognize patterns in insect shape. An AI model trained on 10,000 images of grasshoppers, and 10,000 images of lady beetles will learn overlapping qualities of objects grouped in the same categories. Then, when a new image is shown to the AI model, characteristics of the new image can be used to group the new image into the appropriate category. This allows AI to identify objects in images it has never seen before. </p>
