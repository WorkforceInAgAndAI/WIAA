Drones have different parts that help them to specialize in different tasks. 

Some agricultural drones can carry different cameras and sensors, while others carry tanks which can be used to apply pesticides or fertilizers. 

![Agricultural drone platform](/WIAA/images/solution_drone.jpg)

<div class="impact-callout">
  <h4>Controllers Match the Job</h4>
  <p>Drone controllers also have different parts and functions depending on the connected drone. A drone specializing in sensing and collecting data will have a controller containing data collection functions. On the other hand, drones used for crop input applications will contain functions for dispensing agricultural products. </p>
</div>
