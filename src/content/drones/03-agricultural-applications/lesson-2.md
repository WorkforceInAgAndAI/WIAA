## From Manual Labor to Sensor Data



Drones can be useful for some manual tasks such as broadcasting fertilizers or spraying pesticides. When a drone is equipped with a sensor, the drones can be useful for a wide variety of crop scouting tasks, such as detecting pest outbreaks, estimating crop yield, or mapping field conditions. 

Drones offer many benefits to people in agriculture and other domains, however, there is still room for improvement of drones in agriculture. Understanding the current capabilities of drones in agriculture can help researchers to target their research and development to interests and needs of stakeholders. 

## SWOT: Drones in Agriculture



<div class="swot-grid">

&#x20; <div class="swot-quadrant swot-quadrant--strengths">

&#x20;   <h4>Strengths</h4>

&#x20;   <ul>

&#x20;     <li>Sensor data can be used to detect pests and disease symptoms early. </li>

&#x20;     <li>Decreases time and labor spent on manual data collection.</li>

&#x20;     <li>Decreases intra- and inter-rater variability.</li>

&#x20;   </ul>

&#x20; </div>



&#x20; <div class="swot-quadrant swot-quadrant--weaknesses">

&#x20;   <h4>Weaknesses</h4>

&#x20;   <ul>

&#x20;     <li>Payload capacities, limiting their use for large-scale applications. </li>

&#x20;     <li>Limitations in battery life restrict mission length and capacity. </li>

&#x20;     <li>Agronomic expertise and applicator certification are required for data interpretation and operation.</li>

&#x20;   </ul>

&#x20; </div>



&#x20; <div class="swot-quadrant swot-quadrant--opportunities">

&#x20;   <h4>Opportunities</h4>

&#x20;   <ul>

&#x20;     <li>AI-assisted image analysis is automating pest and plant stress detection. </li>

&#x20;     <li>UAS can be included in integrated farming systems to allow rapid and in-field pest and stress management.</li>

&#x20;   </ul>

&#x20; </div>



&#x20; <div class="swot-quadrant swot-quadrant--threats">

&#x20;   <h4>Threats</h4>

&#x20;   <ul>

&#x20;     <li>Replacing current infrastructure with UAS is expensive.</li>

&#x20;     <li>UAS operation requirements and certification limits user adoption. </li>

&#x20;     <li>Interpretability of data and processing software limits user adoption and accuracy of analysis. </li>

&#x20;   </ul>

## Case Study: Drones in Breeding Research

A plant breeder is someone who addresses challenges to meet the needs of stakeholders. To do this, plant breeders screen many plant genotypes each year, which is time consuming, laborious,  and subject to inter- and intra-rater variability. 

![Iowa Mungbean Diversity Panel](/WIAA/images/IMDP.jpg)
<p class="image-caption">The Iowa Mungbean Diversity Panel from a drone's perspective.</p>

The Iowa Mungbean Diversity Panel, demonstrating the many plant genotypes a plant breeder screen to learn more about the genetics of the crop they work with. To decrease the workload of screening the genotypes in a breeding program, researchers can use UAS equipped with specialized sensors to take measurements from above, decreasing labor, time, and rater variability. 


## Storm Damage Scenario



<div class="scenario-box">

&#x20; <p><strong>You're an agronomist in Iowa.</strong> A large hailstorm just went through and damaged the leaves on your crop. You need to figure out how much damage occurred to decide whether the crop is worth harvesting this year.</p>



&#x20; <details class="lesson-reveal">

&#x20;   <summary>How could a drone help here?</summary>

&#x20;   <p>A drone equipped with a camera or multispectral sensor can quickly cover the whole field and map the extent and severity of leaf damage — much faster than walking every row, and without missing patterns only visible from above.</p>

&#x20; </details>



&#x20; <details class="lesson-reveal">

&#x20;   <summary>What information could the drone collect?</summary>

&#x20;   <p>Aerial images showing defoliation patterns, vegetation index values (to quantify how much healthy leaf area remains), and geolocated damage maps that show which parts of the field were hit hardest.</p>

&#x20; </details>



&#x20; <details class="lesson-reveal">

&#x20;   <summary>What decision might that information support?</summary>

&#x20;   <p>Whether to replant, apply additional inputs to help the crop recover, file a crop insurance claim, or simply proceed to harvest if damage is minor — all decisions that depend on knowing the extent of damage quickly and accurately.</p>

&#x20; </details>

</div>
