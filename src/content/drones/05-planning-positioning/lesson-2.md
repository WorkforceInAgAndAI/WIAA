## Ground Control Points (GCPs)

Before flying, ground control points (GCPs) should be placed in the corners of the area of interest and potentially throughout the field, depending on the size of the field.

![Ground Control Points placed in a field](/WIAA/images/gcps.jpg)
<p class="image-caption">Image captured by a UAV containing GCPs.</p>


<div class="faa-info">
  <p>When GCPs are placed in the field, the GPS position of the GCP needs to be collected. These help to align the images captured during the flight to each other during stitching and enhance the alignment accuracy of the subjects captured in the imagery. GCPs can appear differently, many are simple with black and white squares that can be easily used to precisely pinpoint the same location during GPS point collection in the field and stitching the data later. Other GCPs can be customized to serve specific purposes relevant to diverse imagery-based research. </p>
</div>

## Color Correction

<strong>Color charts</strong> may be placed in the field to be used to calibrate the imagery to a specific known value. 

![Color reference cards used for calibration](/WIAA/images/color_card.jpg)
<p class="image-caption">Color chart for color correction used with RGB imagery.</p>


<strong>Calibration panels</strong> can also be used to correct imagery back to a known value. 

![Calibration panel used to correct drone imagery](/WIAA/images/calibration_pannel.jpg)
<p class="image-caption">Calibration panel for radiometric correction used with multispectral imagery.</p>


<div class="impact-callout">
  <h4>When It Matters Most</h4>
  <p>Color correction is especially useful to do for long flights where lighting conditions may change or when comparisons are wanted between flights 
conducted at different timepoints.  
</p>
</div>
