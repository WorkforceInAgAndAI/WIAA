## Why Drones Matter in Agriculture



Farmers and agriculturalists manage many tasks. Some require hand labor or equipment such as tractors. Others can be completed using drones.



Drones can be useful for manual tasks such as spreading fertilizer or spraying pesticides. When a drone is equipped with a sensor, the drone system becomes useful for a wide variety of crop-scouting tasks — detecting pest outbreaks, estimating crop yield, or mapping field conditions.
