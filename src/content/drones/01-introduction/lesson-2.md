<div class="lesson-intro">
  <p>A drone is a type of unmanned aerial vehicle (UAV) which is capable of movement in Earth’s atmosphere without direct operation of the vehicle from onboard the aircraft (Public Lay 112-95, Section 331 (8) United States). When UAVs are equipped with additional equipment such as sensors, communication tools, controllers, software, and any other attachment or elements remotely controlling drone operation, the UAV is referred to as a Unmanned aerial system (UAS) (Public Lay 112-95, Section 331 (9) United States).<sup>2</sup></p>
</div>

## Key Definitions

<div class="definitions-grid">
  <div class="definition-card">
    <h3>UAV</h3>
    <p class="card-label"><strong>Unmanned Aerial Vehicle:</strong> The drone itself.</p>
  </div>

  <div class="definition-card">
    <h3>UAS</h3>
    <p class="card-label"><strong>Unmanned Aerial System:</strong> The drone, sensor, controller, software, and any other attachments.</p>
  </div>
</div>
