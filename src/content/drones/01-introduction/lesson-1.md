Farmers grow crops over vast spans of land, and farmers need to be able to monitor pests, and crop stresses throughout their fields. But, walking through hundreds of acres of fields can take hours to weeks depending on the level of detail needed, which may delay stress management beyond a point of return. Therefore, researchers have developed aerial tools to view and monitor fields quickly and thoroughly.

## What is Aerial Imagery?

Aerial imagery is collected by cameras and sensors flying or orbiting above Earth’s surface and are used by farmers, and researchers of vast domains to collect data or view vast areas of land such as farm fields, forests, bodies of water, and urban areas.
