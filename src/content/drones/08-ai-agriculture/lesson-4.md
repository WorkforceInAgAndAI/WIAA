## The AI Development Process
AI model training involves multiple steps as follows:


<div class="process-steps">

&#x20; <div class="process-step">

&#x20;   <div class="step-number">1</div>

&#x20;   <div class="step-content"><h3>Data is collected</h3><p>Images, measurements, or observations are gathered.</p></div>

&#x20; </div>

&#x20; <div class="process-step">

&#x20;   <div class="step-number">2</div>

&#x20;   <div class="step-content"><h3>Data is labeled by humans</h3><p>Domain expertsensure the data is accurate and the model can use the labels to learn the meaning of the data.
</p></div>

&#x20; </div>

&#x20; <div class="process-step">

&#x20;   <div class="step-number">3</div>

&#x20;   <div class="step-content"><h3>The model is trained</h3><p>The model uses the labels to learn the meaning of the data.</p></div>

&#x20; </div>

&#x20; <div class="process-step">

&#x20;   <div class="step-number">4</div>

&#x20;   <div class="step-content"><h3>The model is tested</h3><p>Testing confirms the model performs accurately and as expected.</p></div>

&#x20; </div>

&#x20; <div class="process-step">

&#x20;   <div class="step-number">5</div>

&#x20;   <div class="step-content"><h3>The model is deployed</h3><p>The model goes to work in the field on real-world applications.</p></div>

&#x20; </div>

</div>



<p class="cycle-note">Throughout this process AI developers may learn new things about the data, methods used in training, or new technology, so the process forms a development cycle for continual improvements.</p>

## Human-in-the-Loop


Human expertise matters most during the labeling step. Domain experts label data accurately and precisely, so the model trains on quality data; making it far more likely to perform accurately in real-world conditions.



## AI in the Field


AI can be used with drones to detect, classify, and quantify weeds in a field. Finding weeds, identifying their species, and counting them by hand takes hours but AI trained on many weed types can save time.
