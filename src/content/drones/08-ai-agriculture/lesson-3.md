## Multimodal Models

Multimodal models are AI models that can work with multiple data types simultaneously, such as text, images, audio, and video.

![Multimodal AI models](/WIAA/images/multimodal_models.jpg)


<div class="impact-callout">
  <h4>An Example</h4>
  <p>A multimodal model can look at the picture of an insect on a maize leaf and read the question, ‘Is this a pest for maize crop?’, at the same time. It identifies the insect in the image, uses its knowledge about whether that insect harms maize, and then answers the question in text. In this example, the model uses two modalities, namely, image and text, to reach a conclusion.</p>
</div>

![Insect identification example](/WIAA/images/insects.jpg)
<p class="image-caption">AI can be used to help farmers to identify, classify, quantify, and predict pests.</p>


## Combining Tools

These models can work independently, but they become far more useful combined. Vision, classification, and quantification models combined into one system could take an uploaded image and tell you the pest species present *and* how many of each are in the frame.

## Agentic AI Systems

Multimodal models can also be combined with external data to create an **agentic AI system**. Combine vision, classification, and quantification models with external data like air temperature and lifecycle data, and the system could determine, in real time, when a pest species is likely to reproduce next.
