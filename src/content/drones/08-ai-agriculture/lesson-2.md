AI can be used to increase efficiency and automate processes. AI can be used to automate processing imagery collected with drones and then analyzing the processed data. 

<p class="section-intro">AI can quickly detect patterns and trends in the stitched imagery and highlight trends in the data that can be used for rapid management of plant stresses. These detected patterns can lead to the identification, classification, quantification, and prediction of the stress and stress activity. </p>

![Tasks of agriculture AI can support](/WIAA/images/tasks_of_ag.jpg)
<p class="image-caption">The ICQP paradigm for stress phenotyping in plants. AI can help farmers identify the type of stress, level of stress, severity of the stress, and forecast and predict how the stress will act in the future.<sup>13</sup></p>
