We discussed in previous sections how drones can be used to quickly and efficiently scout fields. But how well do drones perform against human experts in the field? 

## The Mission

<p class="section-intro">You are an agronomist and you noticed weeds in your field when you were scouting it earlier today. Before you act against the weeds, you need to know the type of weeds in your field, where the weeds are in your field, and how many weeds are in your field. </p>

Your mission will be completed using two methods. 
	
	
<ul class="interpretation-list">
  <li>Method 1: Manual weed scouting. </li>
  <li>Method 2: Drone-based weed scouting. </li>
</ul>

For each method you and your partner will be responsible for delivering one map for each scouting method, each containing the following information. 

<ul class="interpretation-list">
  <li>The scouting method used</li>
  <li>The location of each "weed" marked on the field map</li>
  <li>A list of weed species (paper colors) found, and the count of each</li>
</ul>

## Field Setup

Six pieces of paper are hidden in the field. Each color represents a different weed species. Your goal is to identify how many of each species are present, and to map where each one was found.

![Field map setup for the scouting mission](/WIAA/images/scouting_fieldmap.jpg)
