Some of the many tasks farmers need to complete can be assisted by satellites and satellite imagery. Satellite imagery is an especially useful tool that can help farmers to identify stressed areas of their fields and help farmers to efficiently apply crop inputs such as water, nutrients, pest management techniques, or pesticides. Using tools such as satellites for precise applications is called precision agriculture, and can help farmers reduce cost, decrease pest resistance, and increase profitability.



<p>Plant breeders use satellite imagery to develop cultivars suited to diverse environmental conditions. Satellites help breeders to distinguish genotypes under different stresses and provide near real-time monitoring of field trials across testing locations. Data is collected across multiple years, can be used to keep track of genotype progression, and overall genotypic population improvement. NASA and other organizations have developed tools and solutions to help farmers grow crops.</p>
