AI is a computer model trained to recognize patterns in data. For example, AI can be trained to recognize patterns in insect shape. By training an AI model with 10,000 images of grasshoppers, and 10,000 images of lady beetles, the AI model will learn that new objects with the same pattern as the objects it was previously called grasshoppers, are also called grasshoppers. This allows AI to identify objects in images it has never seen before.

<p>AI is useful in agriculture by analyzing insects, weeds, and diseases found on hundreds to thousands of acres of farm fields quickly and accurately.</p>
