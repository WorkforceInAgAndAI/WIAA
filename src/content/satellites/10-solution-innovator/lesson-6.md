The appropriate management solution must be decided based on current field conditions, available resources, primary crop, legal regulations, timeliness of application, accuracy needed, cost of technique and the pest needing management.

<p>Tools mounted on tractors such as tillage equipment, or sprayers can be useful for efficient widespread control of pests across fields, however, often are currently limited in precision applications. Precision application tools address precise applications; however, they can be limited in their deployment across large fields.</p>

<p>Some tools have specialized uses and only address one type of pest. Other tools can be used to manage multiple pests during a single application or pass.</p>
