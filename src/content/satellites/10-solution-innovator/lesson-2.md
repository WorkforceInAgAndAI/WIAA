Satellites are tools in space that orbit the Earth collecting data about Earth's surface, atmosphere, and space around Earth. The data from satellites can be used for many different purposes such as agriculture, communication, navigation, security, and weather monitoring.

<p>Satellites carry light sensors that are used to collect images; however, satellites also carry other sensors that are used to collect non-image data such as temperature, wind speed, or gravitational pull. Satellite data can be accessed through organizations who manage the satellite in space.</p>
