Rovers are robots that drive along the ground. Rovers have multiple components such as:

<div class="benefits-grid">
  <div class="benefit-card">
    <div class="benefit-number">1</div>
    <h4>Canopy-Level Cameras</h4>
    <p>Cameras at plant canopy level.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="benefit-card">
    <div class="benefit-number">2</div>
    <h4>Programmable Driving</h4>
    <p>Programmable driving to allow the rover to move on its own.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="benefit-card">
    <div class="benefit-number">3</div>
    <h4>Programmable Scouting</h4>
    <p>Programmable scouting allows the rover to follow the same path at the same time each day.</p>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="impact-callout">
  <h4>Where Rovers Excel</h4>
  <p>Because of these characteristics, rovers can be used in agriculture for tasks such as measuring plant height and structure, monitoring individual plants or leaves, scouting for insects, weeds, and diseases in precise locations, or counting plants, measuring plant density, and yield estimations.</p>
</div>
