<div class="lesson-intro">
  <p>You are a farmer. When you were visiting your 350-acre stage V5 soybean field today you noticed a Japanese beetle infestation in the northeast corner of your field and waterhemp near the field entrance. You do not know if the Japanese beetles and waterhemp are only in the areas where you saw them, or if they have spread to other parts of the field.</p>
  <p>Your goal: Develop a solution to remove the Japanese beetles and waterhemp from your soybean field and determine if you have any other pests in your field. Your success depends on developing a weed management plan that is highly effective, low cost, and timely.</p>
</div>
