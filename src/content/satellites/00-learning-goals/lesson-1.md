By the end of this curriculum, you will be able to:
</div>
<ol>
  <li>Define and explain fundamentals of satellite-based remote sensing and their application to real-world problems in agriculture.</li>
  <li>Describe the major advancements in satellite-based remote sensing and current relevant satellites and resources.</li>
  <li>Define and apply knowledge of data resolution and characteristics to real-world scenarios.</li>
  <li>Understand components of satellites, their interactions, and applications and select suitable satellite tools to solve real-world problems in agriculture.</li>
  <li>Understand ethical data principles to acquisition and analysis of satellite data.</li>
  <li>Use vegetation indices to calculate values to interpret plant health.</li>
  <li>Understand how artificial intelligence can be applied to satellites and their application to agriculture.</li>
  <li>Apply knowledge of satellite platforms, payloads, data processing, and data analysis techniques to solve real-world problems in agriculture.</li>
  <li>Discuss the use of satellites in agriculture and engineering careers.</li>
</ol>
