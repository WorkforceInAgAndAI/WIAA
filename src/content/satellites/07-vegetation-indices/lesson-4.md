Satellites are high above the Earth's surface, which makes achieving the high spatial resolution that researchers require challenging. Often researchers are interested in high spectral resolution, or many narrow bands in the electromagnetic spectrum, but achieving high spectral resolution is often not possible to achieve at the same time as a high spatial resolution.

<p>Achieving high spectral and spatial resolution is not impossible on satellites, it just requires an additional band. This additional band is a panchromatic band, which spans a wide area of the electromagnetic spectrum, so a low spectral resolution, but achieves a high spatial resolution. The high spatial resolution details of the panchromatic band can be combined with the lower spatial resolution multispectral bands to adjust the pixels in the final output.</p>
