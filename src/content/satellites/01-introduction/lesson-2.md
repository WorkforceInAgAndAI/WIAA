Satellites are purpose driven, meaning they are scientific tools built to help humans in a specific way. These satellite tools often serve multiple purposes because they are equipped with a variety of computers, sensors, communication, and power systems that all work together to achieve a product.

<div class="benefits-grid">
  <div class="benefit-card">
    <div class="benefit-number">1</div>
    <h4>Communication</h4>
    <p>Satellites are used to transmit signals between devices on Earth. Phone calls, texts, internet connectivity, and television programming are a few of the primary ways satellites help us communicate. Ground stations on Earth transmit signals to satellites above. The satellites then take those signals and send signals back to Earth amplified across a wide area.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="benefit-card">
    <div class="benefit-number">2</div>
    <h4>Navigation</h4>
    <p>Global positioning systems (GPS) assist navigation and enable precise positioning. These GPS enabled devices pick up signals from satellites orbiting Earth and calculate position, speed, and altitude, of devices on Earth based on the time delay between the GPS receiver and the satellite.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="benefit-card">
    <div class="benefit-number">3</div>
    <h4>Weather and Earth Observation</h4>
    <p>Some satellites carry multiple tools and sensors. Weather satellites carry tools that can be used to detect visible light and infrared radiation from Earth and Earth's atmosphere, which can be used to continuously monitor weather and provide data to make weather forecasts. Data from satellites can be used to reveal information about Earth such as temperature, vegetation, and moisture which can be used for observing characteristics about Earth, and monitoring changes over time. Weather satellites are geostationary, meaning they orbit the Earth at the same rotation speed of Earth, thereby being fixed over the same spot on Earth.<sup>2</sup></p>
  </div>
  <div class="benefit-card">
    <div class="benefit-number">4</div>
    <h4>Security</h4>
    <p>Satellites equipped with high resolution sensors can be used to monitor and track changes across Earth and securely transmit military communications.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="benefit-card">
    <div class="benefit-number">5</div>
    <h4>Agriculture</h4>
    <p>Vegetation reflects light differently depending on plant health. Satellite imagery can capture these wavelengths reflected by the plants and calculate spectral indices which provide proxy measurements of plant health. These plant health metrics help farmers with crop management on a large scale, picking up signals which are hard to identify when manually scouting the field.</p>
  </div>
</div>
