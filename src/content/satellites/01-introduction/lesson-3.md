Earth remote sensing platforms can include platforms grounded on Earth, platforms within Earth's atmosphere, and platforms such as satellites orbiting far outside of Earth's atmosphere. Each platform serves a unique purpose based on its altitude and the sensors it carries.

<p>Satellites provide unique advantages over other types of remote sensing platforms.</p>

<h3>Table 1: Advantages and Disadvantages of Remote Sensing Platforms</h3>

<table class="platform-table">
  <thead>
    <tr>
      <th>Drones</th>
      <th>Planes</th>
      <th>Satellites</th>
    </tr>
  </thead>
  <tbody>
    <tr>
      <td>
        <strong>Advantages</strong>
        <ul>
          <li>High spatial resolution</li>
          <li>Low acquisition and operation costs</li>
          <li>Quick deployment</li>
          <li>Low altitude flights</li>
          <li>Can fly over hazardous areas</li>
        </ul>
      </td>
      <td>
        <strong>Advantages</strong>
        <ul>
          <li>High spatial resolution</li>
          <li>Flexible scheduling</li>
          <li>High payload capacity</li>
          <li>Flies below clouds</li>
          <li>Can cover large areas</li>
        </ul>
      </td>
      <td>
        <strong>Advantages</strong>
        <ul>
          <li>Global coverage</li>
          <li>Regular revisit times</li>
          <li>High operational lifespan</li>
          <li>Low data cost</li>
          <li>Consistent data quality</li>
          <li>Historical data available</li>
        </ul>
      </td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td>
        <strong>Disadvantages</strong>
        <ul>
          <li>Limited flight time and area</li>
          <li>Low payload capacity</li>
          <li>Regulatory regulations</li>
          <li>Highly weather dependent</li>
        </ul>
      </td>
      <td>
        <strong>Disadvantages</strong>
        <ul>
          <li>Expensive</li>
          <li>Limited flight time</li>
          <li>Weather dependent</li>
          <li>Requires airports and flight crews</li>
          <li>Not practical for repeated measurements</li>
        </ul>
      </td>
      <td>
        <strong>Disadvantages</strong>
        <ul>
          <li>Low spatial resolution</li>
          <li>Cloud obstructions in imagery</li>
          <li>Very high launch cost</li>
          <li>Fixed data locations and patterns</li>
          <li>Restricted ability to repair</li>
        </ul>
      </td>
    </tr>
  </tbody>
</table>
