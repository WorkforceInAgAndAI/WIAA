Satellites rotate and move during their orbit around the Earth. Sensors on the satellite are designed to monitor rotation and movement so images captured can be aligned with their position on Earth. The roll, pitch, and yaw define the satellite's movement based three axes of the satellite. Roll defines the rotation of the satellite around its forward-flight axis. Pitch is a measurement of the satellite's rotation around its wing-to-wing axis. Yaw is the rotation of the satellite around its vertical axis.
