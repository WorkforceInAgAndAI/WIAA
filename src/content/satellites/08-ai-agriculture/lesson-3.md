How does AI help us with identification, classification, quantification, and prediction? Special AI tools can be applied to specific tasks.

<h3>Multimodal Models</h3>

<p>Multimodal models are AI models that can work with multiple data types simultaneously, such as text, images, audio, and video. For example, a multimodal model can look at the picture of an insect on a maize leaf and read the question, "Is this a pest for maize crop?", at the same time. It identifies the insect in the image, uses its knowledge about whether that insect harms maize, and then answers the question in text. In this example, the model uses two modalities, namely, image and text, to reach a conclusion.</p>
