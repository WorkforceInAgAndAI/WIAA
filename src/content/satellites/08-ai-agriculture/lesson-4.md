AI model training involves multiple steps as follows:

<div class="benefits-grid">
  <div class="benefit-card">

    <h4>Data Collection</h4>
  </div>
  <div class="benefit-card">
    <div class="benefit-number">2</div>
    <h4>Data Labeling</h4>
    <p>Data is labeled by humans to ensure the data is accurate and the model can use the labels to learn the meaning of the data.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="benefit-card">
    <div class="benefit-number">3</div>
    <h4>Model Training</h4>
    <p>Model is trained on the annotated data.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="benefit-card">
    <div class="benefit-number">4</div>
    <h4>Testing and Validation</h4>
    <p>Model is tested and validated to ensure model is performing accurately.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="benefit-card">
    <div class="benefit-number">5</div>
    <h4>Deployment</h4>
    <p>Model is deployed to be used in the field.</p>
  </div>
</div>

<h3>Cyclical Process</h3>
<p>Throughout this process AI developers may learn new things about the data, methods used in training, or new technology, so the process forms a development cycle for continual improvements.</p>
