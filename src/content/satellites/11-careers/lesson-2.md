If you want to prepare for your career, you can start now. Classes in STEM are great and accessible ways to learn about the world around you and build up knowledge for future classes. You can learn about agriculture by taking agriculture classes, going on farm tours, or getting a job at an agriculture company over the summer. Data analysis skills are also very valuable and needed to understand large amounts of data.

<p>Consider different career options and the college path that will allow you to gain the most information and prepare you for a career of your interest. Skills and knowledge in genetics are useful to know as a data scientist working with genetic data. Mechanical engineering knowledge is useful to know as an agronomist when designing studies implementing new technology.</p>
