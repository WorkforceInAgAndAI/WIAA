Section 4.2 describes spectral resolution and introduces the electromagnetic spectrum as a continuous range of wavelengths of light and the interactions of objects with light. <strong>Light sensors</strong> are tools that detect specific wavelengths of light within certain distributions of the electromagnetic spectrum. The areas of light on the electromagnetic spectrum that sensors detect are called bands.

<p>Scientists can use the data collected from these sensors to determine the health of plants. All plants reflect similar wavelengths of light. However, when plants are stressed, they reflect different quantities of light than healthy plants. Different areas of the electromagnetic spectrum can inform scientists about different aspects of plant health. Because of this, scientists must choose the correct tool to use to gather details that will provide information of interest.</p>

<p>Some of the most used sensors in agriculture are RGB cameras, multispectral cameras, hyperspectral cameras, thermal cameras, and LiDAR sensors. But how are these sensors useful and what is the difference between each sensor?</p>
