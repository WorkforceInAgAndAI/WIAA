Thousands of satellites currently orbit Earth and can be used for a wide array of applications. Some satellites can be used for weather monitoring, others for vegetation tracking, and others for urban monitoring. The various sensors and resolutions on satellites help scientists to determine the best satellite to use for their investigations.

<p>The cost and availability of the satellite data is also a determining factor. Data from some satellites such as Landsat or Sentinel can be accessed free of charge because they are publicly funded. Other satellite data from private companies such as SkySat or Planet Labs require payment or a subscription to access data.</p>

<p>A scientist interested in landscape changes over time may be able to achieve research objectives using freely accessible data. A scientist interested in daily images with high spatial resolution may be required to pay to access data that will meet the research objective.</p>
