Satellite systems are equipped with different sensors that can provide information best suited for different applications. The resolution of sensors varies between different satellites. Some satellites provide high spatial resolution that can be used for detailed field analysis. Other satellites have more coarse spatial resolution; however, they have a higher temporal resolution that provides data more often. The spectral resolution is also an important consideration to ensure the imagery data can be used to extract information needed. Cost of accessing data also varies by satellite with some accessible freely, while others require either a one-time payment or annual subscriptions.

<p>When requesting satellite data, the area of interest (AOI) must be defined spatially and temporally. Coordinates of AOI corners provide the area, while start and end dates of data acquisition provide a range of data to gather. Data over multiple times can be very useful and is highly reliable because of the satellite's consistent orbital pattern.</p>
