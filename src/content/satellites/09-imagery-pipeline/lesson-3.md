Many atmospheric obstructions or particles suspended throughout the atmosphere, fall between satellites and the Earth's surface. These particles decrease image quality. Depending on the type of atmospheric obstruction, different corrective techniques can be used to adjust the image quality to more accurately represent ground truth.
