<div class="definitions-grid">
  <div class="definition-card">
    <h3>Landsat</h3>
    <p>Launched in 1972, this is the oldest satellite imaging system providing the public with free 30-meter resolution imagery for 50 years. Landsat 8 and 9 are currently in orbit allowing an 8-day temporal resolution. Landsat imagery is available freely on the USGS EarthExplorer.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="definition-card">
    <h3>Sentinel</h3>
    <p>Ten-meter spatial resolution is available freely on the Copernicus Data Space Explorer collected by Sentinel-2. This imagery has a temporal resolution of 10 days across Earth and has been collecting imagery since 2015.<sup>9</sup></p>
  </div>
  <div class="definition-card">
    <h3>SkySat</h3>
    <p>Operated commercially by Planet Labs, this constellation of satellites provides 0.57-to-1.0-meter resolution. Near real-time crop and land monitoring is available at the cost of a paid subscription.<sup>10</sup></p>
  </div>
  <div class="definition-card">
    <h3>Pleiades Neo</h3>
    <p>Airbus commercially operates a constellation of satellites with 0.3-to-1.2-meter resolution. Near real-time monitoring is also available to users by paid subscriptions.</p>
  </div>
</div>
